Effective Variant Screening

Variant primary screening

Materials and Apparatus:

Chloramphenicol (34 mg/mL)
Ampicillin (100 mg/mL)
20% (w/v) L-arabinose solution
50% (w/v) glucose solution
LB liquid medium
LB agar plates supplemented with Cm, Amp and 0.5% (w/v) glucose
Escherichia coli competent cells harbouring plasmids pEYFP-CTAC, pEYFP-ATAC, pEYFP-TTAC, pEYFP-GTAC, pEYFP-CAAC, pEYFP-CCAC, pEYFP-CGAC, pEYFP-CTTC, pEYFP-CTCC, pEYFP-CTGC
Tecan Infinite 200 Pro microplate reader
DragonLab D1524R centrifuge

Protocol:

1. Prepare a 10 μL Gibson assembly system containing 5 μL Gibson enzyme, 16 ng insert fragment and 80 ng corresponding spacer backbone. Place the mixture on ice immediately after the reaction.
2. Transform 10 μL assembly product into Escherichia coli competent cells harboring pEYFP-TAM plasmid. Transform 1 μL pSTING-E1/2/3 plasmid as the negative control. Transform 1 μL pSTING-E1 plasmid into Escherichia coli competent cells harboring pEYFP-origin plasmid as the positive control. Concentrate the bacterial suspension to 100 μL and spread onto agar plates, then incubate overnight at 37 °C for 12 h.
3. Pick single colonies from plates and inoculate into sterile culture tubes containing LB medium supplemented with Cm, Amp and 0.5% (w/v) glucose, including positive and negative controls. Incubate the tubes overnight for 14 h at 37 °C with shaking at 200 r/min.
4. Inoculate bacterial cultures at 2% (v/v) into fresh LB liquid medium, and incubate for 3 h at 37 °C with shaking at 200 r/min. Set three biological replicates for each sample.
5. Transfer 200 μL of bacterial culture to measure OD and EYFP fluorescence intensity, then add 28 μL of 20% (w/v) L-arabinose for induction. Incubate for 6 h at 37 °C with shaking at 200 r/min.
6. After 6 h of induction, take 200 μL bacterial culture to measure OD value and EYFP fluorescence intensity.
7. Inoculate bacterial cultures at 2% (v/v) into LB liquid medium supplemented with Cm, Amp and 0.5% (w/v) glucose. Incubate for 3 h at 37 °C with shaking at 200 r/min, then take 200 μL culture for OD measurement.
8. Select variants whose fluorescence signal is lower than that of the negative control for subsequent experiments.

Plasmid Interference for Rescreening

Materials and Apparatus:

Escherichia coli competent cells harboring pSC101-TAM
Variant plasmids
SOC liquid medium, with glucose replaced by 0.2% L-arabinose
Kanamycin (50 mg/mL)
Chloramphenicol (25 mg/mL)
DragonLab D1524R centrifuge
6 × 6 square agar plate

Protocol:

1. Transform 100 ng variant plasmids into Escherichia coli competent cells harboring pSC101-TAM and 100 ng pSTING-E1/2/3 plasmids as the negative control; transform 100 ng pSTING-E1 into Escherichia coli competent cells harboring pSC101-CTAC as the positive control. For recovery, add 1000 μL SOC medium in which glucose was replaced by L-arabinose, and incubate for 3 h at 37 °C with shaking at 200 r/min.
2. Concentrate the bacterial suspension to 50 μL, and perform serial dilutions of 0, 10-1, 10-2, 10-3, 10-4, 10-5 using SOC medium with glucose substituted by L-arabinose.
3. Spot 5 μL bacterial suspension onto each grid of the square plate according to dilution gradients. Prepare two replicates for each sample.
4. Incubate the plates in a 37 °C incubator for 24 h, then perform colony counting.
